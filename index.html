How this is calculated

Cancelled, returned, and refunded orders are excluded. Product sales use saved order-item line totals; product costs use each order item's saved unit cost, so later product cost changes do not rewrite historical estimates. Delivery fees and general operating expenses are not included. This is gross profit, not net profit.
